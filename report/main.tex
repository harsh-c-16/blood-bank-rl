\begin{abstract}
We formulate platelet inventory management in a hospital blood bank as a Markov decision process and train PPO and DQN agents to balance shortages, expiry, and holding costs. Across 30 evaluation seeds of 365 days each, 1M-step multi-group PPO approaches the tuned base-stock reward while reducing expiry, and longer single-group PPO training yields a similar reward to base-stock. DQN remains slightly behind the heuristic, showing that training setup and action-space scale affect performance.
\end{abstract}
